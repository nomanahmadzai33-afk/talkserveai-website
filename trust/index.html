Trust & Privacy

How we actually handle your data

Plain language, no marketing badges we haven't earned. We are not SOC 2 or ISO certified — here's exactly what we do instead.

What we collect

When you contact us or use our demo, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and restaurant details you voluntarily provide. For live restaurant clients, guest reservation details (name, party size, contact info, requested time) are collected so the AI can complete a booking.

How it's used

Information is used solely to respond to inquiries, run the AI receptionist service for our clients, and improve the product. We do not sell guest or client data to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it goes

Calls and messages are processed through two well-known infrastructure providers: Twilio (phone and SMS) and OpenAI (the AI that understands and responds). Reservation data may also sync to Google Calendar or SevenRooms when a restaurant uses those integrations. We don't share data with anyone beyond what's necessary to operate the service.

Storage

Demo chat conversations on this website are not stored. Contact form submissions are stored securely and only accessible to the TalkServe AI team.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for basic site functionality only — no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing talkserveai@gmail.com.

💬 We're a small, hands-on team — if you have specific data-handling or security questions before signing up, email us directly and we'll answer honestly, including where we don't yet have a formal process in place.
